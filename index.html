  • Compared ENN, SMOTE, SMOTE-ENN, SMOTE-Tomek
  • Best: ENN + Gradient Boosting → Recall 71.7%, AUC 0.791
  • ~14% recall improvement over baseline
  • Voting ensemble stabilized: Recall 71.4%, AUC 0.789
  • Key predictors: Age, BMI, Blood Pressure
